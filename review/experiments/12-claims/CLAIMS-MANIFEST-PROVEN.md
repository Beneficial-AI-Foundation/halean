Rule for the verdict on a `proven` entry:

* `correct`: the cited theorems fix the function's result, or the note claims no more than the theorems state and a theorem pins the behaviour the status claims.
* `overstated`: the cited theorems give properties or edge cases only.
* `correct for the wrong reason`: `proven` is justified by a theorem the note does not cite.

Every cited theorem exists in `HALean/` (checked by the script against all `theorem` declarations).
Every `proven` entry was judged by at least one reviewer; 13 were judged by more than one, and for 6 of those the reviewers differ. I read the theorems for those 6 and decided.
The column "theorems the note cites" is extracted from `notes:` by the script.
