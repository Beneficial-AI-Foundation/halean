# Side-by-side reading: automation engine

Upstream paths are under `upstream/ha-core/homeassistant/` (tag 2026.10.4). Lean paths are under `HALean/Automation/`
at the reviewed commit. Line numbers are from those files. "AUT-n" refers to `FINDINGS.md`.
The manifest maps 211 `lean:` lines to `HALean.Automation.*`; they are grouped here by upstream function.
